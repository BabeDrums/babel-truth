## Ownership rule

Babel Truth owns cross-app rules. This repository owns its app/prototype-specific behavior.

Do not silently overwrite a local difference merely because Truth differs. Classify the conflict first:

- stale divergence → adopt Truth
- intentional experiment → preserve and label the divergence
- potential upstream improvement → surface it before changing either side
- app-local exception → preserve locally and clarify ownership

Update the recorded Truth commit only after reconciliation is complete.
